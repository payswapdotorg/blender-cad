## 2. Where planegcs is strong

- **Exact DOF accounting on every mapping case** — including the arc: the
  corpus's 5-DOF arc expectation (center+radius+2 angles+2 endpoints minus
  4 ArcRules equations) is planegcs's NATIVE parameterization.
- **Correct redundancy attribution on the fixture that rejected slvs** —
  `rect_redundant_duplicate_dimension`: planegcs reports **dof 0 +
  redundant=[k6] (the duplicate Vertical)** — the W1-correct diagnosis slvs
  could not produce (slvs named `d1`, a non-redundant constraint, from
  Newton's final iterate).
- **Correct conflict attribution** — `conflicting_distances` names BOTH d1
  and d2; `coincident_plus_distance` names k1+d1.
- **Bitwise failure semantics** — copy-solve + applySolution-on-Success: every
  failed case leaves the input geometry bitwise untouched (the same
  observable as SolidCraft's explicit restore; slvs had the same property).
- **Determinism** — bitwise in-process (5×) and cross-process (3×,
  `PLANEGCS_HASH 61a28ac5d660149c 0b5d4f0fee186525 dd7ba596bb9d6df4 ok=1`).
- **Native value mutation without rebuild** — constraint datums are
  caller-owned `double*`: set + initSolution + solve on the SAME System.
  Verified in both edit cases (landing on the exact analytic branches) and
  the staircase edit (49 ms mutation path vs 130 ms rebuild path at N=200);
  slvs had NO mutation API (the m3-eval rejection's pillar 2).
- **Diagnostics surface** — dofsNumber + conflicting/redundant/partially-
  redundant tags + dependent-parameters groups (GCS.h:608-651).

## 3. The honest failures (all decision-relevant)

### 3.1 rect_redundant_duplicate_dimension — correct diagnosis, no solve

Even with FreeCAD's FULL 4-step chain (including the SQP-augmented last
resort, Sketch.cpp:4860-4875 — temporary equality pins on every parameter +
two-subsystem SQP), planegcs returns **Converged** (minimized-not-zeroed)
from every step: no write-back, corners stay at the rough input. So:

| solver | solve | diagnosis |
|---|---|---|
| SolidCraft (W1 oracle) | **solved, exact corners** | redundant=[k8/k6], implied-by named |
| slvs (m3-eval) | failed | bad list MISATTRIBUTES (names d1) |
| **planegcs (this lane)** | **failed** | **redundant=[k6] — correctly named, dof 0** |

This is consistent with FreeCAD's real-world behavior class (redundant
sketches are reported and the solver refuses to fully solve; users are told
to remove the redundant constraint). **Consequence for the gate:** planegcs
diagnoses better than slvs but still fails to SOLVE the exactly-redundant
fixture that SolidCraft solves.

### 3.2 slot_constrained — solved at ~2.5e-5; diagnose misattribution

Solved via DogLeg to the expected obround **within ~2.5e-5** — but the
corpus's 1e-6 CHECK_TOL fails 4 position checks (cap x-offsets 1.6e-5..2.5e-5:
the DogLeg stopped at its convergence threshold; planegcs's residual scaling
reaches the tolerance without zeroing the positions to 1e-6). Additionally
planegcs's diagnose flags 4 constraints (2 ArcRules + 2 tangencies) as
"conflicting" and reports dof 2 for this formulation.

The **formulation probes** (envelope `formulation_probes`, driver mode
`--slotdebug`) document the full picture:

- **A: AngleViaPoint + structural sharing** (FreeCAD's endpoint-tangency
  form): structurally the W1 slot is dof 0 — but planegcs reports dof 4 +
  10 flagged (order/formulation-dependent diagnosis), and the SOLVE fails.
- **B: separate endpoints + Coincidents + AngleViaPoint** (FreeCAD's own
  drawing pattern): dof 4 + d1 flagged; solve failed (or collapses to the
  r→0 degenerate manifold — the atan2(0,0)=0 property of AngleViaPoint at
  zero-length normals — observed in an earlier probe run).
- **C: curve-level Line-Arc tangent (P2LDistance form) + structural sharing**
  (the corpus formulation): **solves to the expected obround** — chosen for
  the corpus record; its diagnose still reports dof 2 + 4 flagged.

**Consequence:** planegcs's diagnose is *formulation- and order-dependent*
on degenerate/seamed systems (same fixture: dof 0 vs 2 vs 4 across
formulations/orders), and its endpoint-tangency form has a degenerate
manifold the rough W1 seed falls into. The corpus formulation (C) is the
robust one and is what the envelope records.

### 3.3 triangle_edit_unsolvable — no diagnostics on a clean-structured failure

d1=55 is structurally consistent (dof 0, no conflicts) but numerically
unsolvable (discriminant<0). planegcs's diagnose is CLEAN (correct — there
is no structural conflict), the full 4-step chain fails, the geometry is
restored bitwise — but the failure carries **no named constraints** (slvs
named its final-iterate violators; SolidCraft names k3/k4/k5 from its
failure surface). The `bad_list_nonempty` declared check FAILS honestly:
planegcs exposes no per-failure diagnostic surface for this failure class.

## 4. Scale (staircase; 5 fresh processes per N; medians)

| N | planegcs solve (s) | slvs (m3-eval, same fixture) | SolidCraft (h2h-3, same fixture) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 2.09e-04 | 1.9e-04 | 7.64e-05 |
| 50 | 2.20e-03 | 8.4e-04 | 1.74e-04 |
| 100 | 1.18e-02 | 3.05e-03 | 2.73e-04 |
| 200 | **1.06e-01** | 1.61e-02 | **4.84e-04** |

At N=200 planegcs is ~6.6× slower than slvs and ~220× slower than SolidCraft
on the same staircase (dof 0, len err ~1.5e-12 everywhere). The growth is
super-linear (dense-Jacobian Newton on the full system; no decomposition
reuse — `initSolution` re-partitions on every solve, which is also why the
"mutation path" at N=200 costs 49 ms: the re-init + full solve, vs the 1.3 ms
warm re-solve that skips nothing but is measured after the system is already
at the solution). **The scale dimension is the most one-sided row of the
head-to-head.**

## 5. API-surface facts (citations at the pin)

- Mutation without rebuild: native (datums are caller-owned `double*`);
  contrast slvs rebuild-only (m3-eval §4.1 pillar 2).
- Diagnostics: `dofsNumber()`, `getConflicting/getRedundant/getPartiallyRedundant`
  (TAGS — caller maps labels), `getDependentParams` (GCS.h:608-651).
- Diagnose caveats: formulation/order-dependent on seamed systems (§3.2
  probes — recorded with full evidence in the envelope).
- Failure semantics: reference/copy + applySolution-on-Success
  (GCS.cpp:1849-1888, Sketch.cpp:4823-4826) — bitwise restore.
- No named-constraint surface on structurally-clean numerical failures (§3.3).
- Solver chain: 4 steps including SQP-augmented (Sketch.cpp:4860-4875) — this
  lane implements it verbatim; no FreeCAD layer above planegcs was exercised
  (Sketch.cpp/SketchObject.cpp were read as the mapping reference and cited).
