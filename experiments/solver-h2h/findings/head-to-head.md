## 2. Reading of the evidence

**SolidCraft-solver wins every measured dimension of this gate.**

1. **Conformance:** it is the only candidate that reproduces TL2's full W1
   record AND passes the fixture that rejected slvs (solved + named the
   duplicate). Its only failures are two NEW coverage probes' DOF checks,
   caused by the LITERAL mapping of zero-valued `distance_x/y` onto the
   crate's unsigned form (rank analysis blind at dp=0) — with the
   crate-native remedy (`HorizontalPoints`/`VerticalPoints`) probe-verified
   to dof 0 + exact positions. That is an adapter-level seam note, not a
   solver defect.
2. **PlaneGCS's honest failures are decision-relevant, not noise:** the
   exactly-redundant fixture does not solve even through FreeCAD's full
   4-step chain (a real-world FreeCAD behavior class), the slot converges
   only to ~2.5e-5 with a formulation-dependent diagnosis (probes recorded),
   and structurally-clean numerical failures carry no diagnostics.
3. **Scale is the most one-sided row:** 0.48 ms vs 106.3 ms at N=200 on the
   identical staircase (~220×); SolidCraft's decomposition (union-find over
   constraint blocks, block-diagonal Jacobian) vs planegcs's full-system
   dense Newton with re-partitioning on every solve.
4. **Where they tie:** determinism (bitwise, both), failure semantics
   (bitwise restore, both), and value mutation (both have native paths;
   SolidCraft adds continuation substeps).
5. **License:** both are now VERIFIED at their pins (this lane cleared the
   PlaneGCS (verify) and the Eigen@3147391d blocker): SolidCraft = MIT OR
   Apache-2.0, 12-crate closure, zero GPL, no native code; PlaneGCS =
   LGPL-2.1-family (per-file legacy wording recorded verbatim) + Eigen
   MPL-2.0 + boost at pack time. Both are GPL-3.0-or-later-host-compatible
   for combined distribution; neither license REJECTS; the obligations
   differ in kind (permissive vs LGPL library-boundary + notice retention).
6. **Packaging:** both bundle a native artifact into the extension and both
   hit the SAME still-unverified extension-platform policy gate
   ([packaging-review.md](packaging-review.md) — h2h-5). SolidCraft's
   packaging question is a Rust cdylib cross-compilation toolchain;
   PlaneGCS's is a C++ vendored-source build + LGPL/Eigen/boost notice
   compliance + the same policy.
