cross-solver semantic divergence, not a solver defect.** The declared-checks

## 3. The honest failures: unsigned distances at zero (2 declared dof checks)

`parallel_offset` and `perpendicular_pair` (both NEW coverage probes) fail their
`dof_0` declared checks: the LITERAL cases.json mapping of a zero-valued
`distance_x`/`distance_y` onto the crate's **unsigned** `DistanceX`/`DistanceY`
(residual `|dp|.axis − value`, solver.rs:544-545) hides the constraint from the
finite-difference rank analysis at `dp = 0` (`|h| − |−h| = 0`): the solver
reports `dof 1` and flags the constraint — **while the geometry still lands on
the exact expected positions** (residual 0.0; all 8 expected coordinates
reached, from the constructive seed).

The crate's NATIVE same-row/column kinds (`HorizontalPoints`/`VerticalPoints`,
signed residuals solver.rs:462-465) express the same constraint exactly —
verified by a remedy probe recorded in the envelope: **dof 0 + exact
positions**. Recorded as a cross-solver semantic divergence with an
adapter-level remedy (our seam layer would emit the native kind), not as a
solver failure; nothing was faked, no case was dropped.

## 4. API surface facts (citations at the pin)

- **32 ConstraintKind variants** (model.rs); this corpus exercises 16 (envelope
  `constraint_coverage_matrix` maps every PAR kind to its crate API + citation).
- **Value mutation WITHOUT rebuild**: `ConstraintKind::set_value`
  (model.rs:370-389) + `solve_after_value_edit` (solver.rs:1369-1371) with
  deterministic continuation substeps (≤64, old→new) when the one-jump solve
  fails or folds — the capability slvs lacks (the m3-eval rejection's pillar 2).
- **No persistent cross-solve dirty cache** in the crate (component-level
  decomposition is per solve, structural; union-find over constraint blocks,
  solver.rs:1930-1966) — a document-model concern, i.e. our seam's concern.
- **Diagnostics**: per-constraint residuals, iteration counts, max-residual,
  per-entity determined flags, implied-by relationships, fold detection.
- **Determinism design**: no RNG anywhere in the closure; fixed LM schedule
  (λ0=1e-3, ÷3 on success, ×4 on rejection, caps 1e-12/1e12); deterministic
  work UNITS not wall-clock budgets (solver.rs:956-957).
